## 1. Purpose

This specification states the acceptance criteria for incoming ingredient lots, and the analytical
methods by which a result is considered reportable. A lot is acceptable when every attribute in §2
is within its stated limits and was determined by a reportable method for that attribute.

## 2. Acceptance criteria

| Attribute | Limits | Unit | Reportable method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assay | 99.0 – 101.0 | % | HPLC (SPEC-7 M-01) |
| Moisture | 0.0 – 0.5 | % | Karl Fischer (SPEC-7 M-04) |
| Heavy Metals | 0.0 – 10.0 | ppm | ICP-MS (SPEC-7 M-11) |
| Bulk Density | 0.45 – 0.75 | g/mL | USP <616> (SPEC-7 M-21) |

Results shall be expressed in the unit stated above. Where a certificate expresses a result in a
different unit, the result shall be converted before comparison against the limits, and the
conversion recorded.

## 3. Methods

### 3.1 Reportable methods

A result is reportable only when produced by the method named in §2 for that attribute. A result
produced by any other method is indicative and shall not be used to determine acceptance.

### 3.2 Moisture — determination

Karl Fischer titration (M-04) is the sole reportable method for moisture.

Loss on drying (M-03) is retained in this specification as an indicative method only. Loss on
drying does not distinguish water from other volatiles and over-reads on hygroscopic materials,
where it has been observed to return values materially above the Karl Fischer determination on the
same sample. A loss-on-drying result may be recorded for information. It shall not be used to
release a lot.

### 3.3 Method changes

A change to a reportable method requires a revision to this specification and a controlled
notification to suppliers. Suppliers are given ninety days from notification to transition.

## 4. Certificates citing a withdrawn revision

Where a supplier's certificate cites a withdrawn revision of this specification, or reports an
attribute by a method that is not reportable under §2, the lot shall be referred to Quality. It
shall not be accepted on the strength of the certificate alone.

## 5. Non-conforming results

A result outside the limits in §2 is non-conforming irrespective of any conformance statement made
by the supplier on the certificate. Where a certificate states conformance and the stated result is
outside the limits, the certificate is in error and the lot shall be referred to Quality.
